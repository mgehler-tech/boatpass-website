PRIVACY Privacy Policy This privacy policy applies to the BoatPass app for Android (Google Play) and iPhone/iPad (App Store) and to the website boatpass.de. Last updated: October 2026. The German version is legally binding. 01 Controller Marius Gehler Blissestraße 56 · 10713 Berlin · Germany support@boatpass.de WEBSITE 02 Hosting This website is hosted by Netlify Inc. (2325 3rd Street, Suite 215, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit the website, technically necessary data (IP address, access time, browser) is stored in Netlify server logs. Delivery runs through the content delivery network of Cloudflare Inc. (101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Cloudflare processes technically necessary connection data (including the IP address) to deliver the website securely and quickly and to protect it against attacks. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation) 03 Website analytics This website uses Plausible Analytics – a privacy-friendly tool without cookies and without collecting personal data. No consent banner is required. It counts page views and clicks on store links (Google Play, App Store), without any personal reference. plausible.io/data-policy → 04 Contact form The contact form is processed by Formspree (Formspree Inc., USA). Submitted data (name, email address, message) is used solely to answer your request and deleted afterwards. It is not passed on to third parties. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures / answering requests) APPS (ANDROID & IOS) 05 Principle: local data storage BoatPass stores learning progress, settings and purchase status only locally on your device. There is no user account and no sync via our servers. This applies equally to the Android and the iOS app. · Learning progress (answered questions, error rates, streaks) – stored locally in the app, never leaves your device. · Settings (language, notification times, daily goal) – stored locally in the app, never leaves your device. · Purchase status (which catalogues were bought) – stored locally in the app, never leaves your device. · Exam history (results of past mock exams) – stored locally in the app, never leaves your device. 06 Analytics and crash data (Firebase) – only with consent We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. Both are switched off by default and only become active once you consent. Without consent the app works fully. Firebase Analytics collects: screens and features used, device type, OS version, language setting, app version, install source, session length and a random app instance ID. If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends the stack trace, error message, device state (memory, OS, app version) and a random installation ID. Learning content, answers and purchase data are not transmitted. We collect no advertising ID – neither the Android Advertising ID nor Apple's IDFA – and do not track you for advertising. That is also why the iPhone app never asks for permission to track you across apps. For users in the EEA the provider is Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), acting as processor under Art. 28 GDPR. Transfers to Google LLC in the USA are possible and are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). According to Google, Firebase Analytics does not store IP addresses. More information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy Legal basis: consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). Retention: analytics event data 2 months, crash reports 90 days. Give or withdraw consent: in the app under More → "Share analytics data". Withdrawal takes effect immediately for the future. 07 Loading question corrections So that corrections to the question catalogue arrive without an app update, the app downloads a corrections file from cdn.boatpass.app at startup. This necessarily transmits your IP address and the time of the request to the delivering server. No user or device ID is sent, and we do not analyse these requests on a personal basis. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in correct, up-to-date exam questions). 08 Notifications If you enable study reminders, local notifications are scheduled on your device. No push messages are sent via external servers. You can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings. ANDROID APP ONLY 09 Purchases via Google Play Purchases in the Android app are processed via Google Play Billing. Google's privacy policy applies (policies.google.com/privacy). We receive no payment data; Google only tells us whether a valid purchase exists. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). 10 Promo codes (Firebase Remote Config) When you redeem a promo code in the Android app, the app checks it via Firebase Remote Config (Google Ireland Limited). A random installation ID is transmitted so the configuration can be delivered. No personal data such as your name or email address is transmitted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (redeeming the promo code at your request). IOS APP ONLY (IPHONE & IPAD) 11 Purchases via the App Store Downloads and purchases in the iOS app are processed via the App Store. For users in the EU, the contracting party for payment is Apple Distribution International Ltd. (Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland). Apple's privacy policy applies (apple.com/legal/privacy). We receive no payment data and no Apple ID; via StoreKit the app only learns whether a valid purchase exists. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). 12 Statistics from Apple Apple provides us with aggregated statistics in App Store Connect, such as downloads, App Store page views and crash counts. Usage and crash data are only included if you agreed to share them with app developers in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. We only receive this data in aggregated form and cannot link it to any person. GENERAL 13 Sharing data with third parties Data is shared only within the services described above: Google (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics after consent, Remote Config, Google Play Billing), Apple (App Store), the server for question corrections, Netlify and Cloudflare for operating the website, and Formspree for the contact form. There is no further sharing with third parties. We do not sell data. 14 Data deletion All data stored locally in the app is deleted when you uninstall it. You can delete a catalogue's learning progress at any time via More → Reset progress. Purchased catalogues stay tied to your Google or Apple account and can be recovered after reinstalling via "Restore purchases". Firebase analytics data is pseudonymous and not linked to a name; withdrawing consent stops further collection. For other deletion requests: support@boatpass.de 15 Your rights (GDPR) Under the GDPR you have the right to: ✓ Access your stored data (Art. 15) ✓ Rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16) ✓ Erase your data (Art. 17) ✓ Restrict processing (Art. 18) ✓ Data portability (Art. 20) ✓ Object to processing (Art. 21) ✓ Withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)) ✓ Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77) Competent supervisory authority: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit (BlnBDI), Friedrichstr. 219, 10969 Berlin, Germany.
