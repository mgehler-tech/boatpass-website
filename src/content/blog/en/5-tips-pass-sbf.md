The German boating license isn't rocket science — but without the right preparation, the exam can still go wrong. These five tips make the difference.

## 1. Study the complete question catalog

A common mistake: only studying the "hard" categories. The exam draws from all areas. With the Boatpass app, you always have all 400+ questions with you.

## 2. Practice under real exam conditions

Use the app's exam simulation mode: 30 questions, time limit, no instant feedback. This gets you used to real exam pressure.

## 3. Focus on your weak spots

After each practice session, you'll see which categories need more work. The weak spot training feature shows you exactly those questions again — more efficient learning.

## 4. Study regularly, not in blocks

30 minutes daily beats 4 hours on the weekend. The brain processes information better in small, regular portions. Make studying a daily habit.

## 5. Book your exam date early

Exam slots fill up quickly. Register before you start studying — it also gives you a concrete deadline.

## Conclusion

With consistent preparation and the right tools, passing the SBF is very achievable. Start for free with the Boatpass app.
