Every year, hundreds of candidates fail the SBF theory exam — even though they studied enough. The same mistakes keep appearing.

## Mistake 1: Not practicing exam simulations

Many people study individual questions but never practice under real exam conditions. The real exam has a time limit and no instant feedback. Going in unprepared for this can cause nerves and careless errors.

**Solution:** Complete at least 3–5 full mock exams before your real exam.

## Mistake 2: Memorizing instead of understanding

Memorizing answers fails when questions are slightly rephrased. Exam questions can vary.

**Solution:** Read the explanations for wrong answers — every question in the Boatpass app includes a clear explanation.

## Mistake 3: Underestimating lights and sound signals

This category has many similar-looking details with different meanings. Many candidates study it too superficially.

**Solution:** Use spaced repetition — the app automatically shows weak questions more frequently.

## Mistake 4: Registering too late

Exam slots are often booked weeks in advance. Waiting until you feel "ready" is waiting too long.

**Solution:** Book your exam date before you start studying. A deadline motivates.

## Mistake 5: Ignoring the practical exam

Many focus only on theory and barely practice for the practical exam. Yet the practical often causes the most stress.

**Solution:** Combine theory study with practical water exercises — even without a formal course.
