The Sportbootführerschein (SBF) is the official boating license for motorized vessels in Germany. It is required for boats with more than 15 HP on federal waterways.

## What does the exam cover?

The theory exam consists of 30 questions from the official DMYV question catalog. You need at least 24 correct answers to pass.

Topics include:
- **Navigation** – traffic rules, signs, lights
- **Seamanship** – knots, maneuvers, anchoring
- **Maritime law** – collision regulations
- **Weather** – weather patterns, hazards
- **Safety** – equipment, distress signals
- **Engine knowledge** – propulsion, fuel

## How does the exam work?

1. Register with the DMYV or an accredited club
2. Theory exam (multiple choice, 30 questions, 60 minutes)
3. Practical exam on the water

## What does it cost?

Exam fees range from €100–200 depending on the club. Add course fees of €150–400 if you take a preparation course.
