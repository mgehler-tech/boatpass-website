Since October 2026 BoatPass has also been on the **App Store**. That means you can now study for the German recreational boating licence (Sportbootführerschein, SBF) and the marine radio certificates on your iPhone and iPad, with the same official question catalogue and the same study modes as in the Android app. Here is what the app does, what it costs and what to watch out for when you buy.

## A real iOS app, not a copy

BoatPass for iPhone and iPad is a standalone app built with Apple's own tools. It feels the way you expect an iOS app to feel and still does everything the Android version does: the same catalogues, the same study modes, the same 15 free questions per day. If you switch from Android to iPhone, or study with friends on different devices, you will feel at home straight away.

## What the app does

### Every official question

BoatPass contains the complete official question catalogue, valid for 2026 exams:

- **SBF Binnen** (inland, motor and sail): 300 questions
- **SBF See** (coastal): 285 questions and 15 navigation tasks
- **UBI** (inland VHF radio): 130 questions
- **SRC** (Short Range Certificate): 180 questions
- **LRC** (Long Range Certificate): 76 questions

You can see exactly which questions come up in the exam on our pages for the [SBF Binnen exam questions](/en/sbf-inland-exam-questions/) and the [SBF See exam questions](/en/sbf-coastal-exam-questions/).

### Every question explained

Each question comes with a short **explanation** and a **memory aid**. So you learn not only which answer is right but also why. That helps most with questions that are worded slightly differently in the exam from how you remember them.

### Exams under real conditions

In exam mode you practise with the **official exam papers** or with randomly mixed papers. The number of questions, the time limit and the pass mark match the real exam. Afterwards you see straight away which topics are still shaky. How the exam itself works is covered in our overview of the [exam process](/en/sbf-exam/).

### Study plan and exam readiness

Enter your exam date and BoatPass plans your **daily goal** up to that day. **Exam readiness** shows how many questions you really know for sure, not just how many you have seen once. Reminders and a small rank system from ship's boy to admiral help you keep going.

### Navigation and knots

For the SBF See there are all **15 navigation tasks** with a step-by-step solution. For the practical exam you will find the **10 sailor's knots** with pictures.

### In English and German, also offline

All questions, explanations and memory aids are available in **English** and German. You can switch at any time in the settings, which also helps you learn the German wording used in the exam. With full access the app works **completely offline**, including all images, so it also works in a harbour without signal.

## What the app costs on iPhone

The download is **free**. Without buying anything you can study 15 questions a day and take one mock exam. You do not need an account or a sign-up.

Full access is a **one-off purchase per licence**; there is no subscription. These prices apply on the App Store (as of October 2026):

- **SBF Binnen**: €9.99
- **SBF See**: €9.99
- **UBI**: €8.99
- **SRC**: €8.99
- **LRC**: €7.99
- **SBF combo bundle** (Binnen and See): €16.99
- **Radio bundle** (UBI, SRC and LRC): €17.99

Some prices differ slightly from Google Play, because Apple only allows fixed price tiers. You can find the current prices for both stores on our [homepage](/en/#pricing). What the boating licence costs overall, including exam fees, is covered in our overview of [SBF costs](/en/sbf-costs/).

## Important: purchases apply per store

If you have used BoatPass on Android so far, there is one thing you should know: **a Google Play purchase does not unlock anything on iPhone**, and vice versa. Google and Apple process purchases completely separately, and BoatPass deliberately has no user account of its own. Your learning progress stays locally on your device and is not synced via our servers.

Within one platform this is no problem: on a new iPhone or an iPad you recover your purchases with the same Apple ID via **"Restore purchases"** in the settings.

## How to find the right app

The App Store also lists an **app with the same name from a US boat rental service** in Florida, which has nothing to do with us. Our study app is the one by **Marius Gehler** in the **Education** category.

The easiest way is [boatpass.de/en/app](/en/app/): the page recognises your device and sends you straight to the right store, so the App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android.

## On iPad too

BoatPass also runs on **iPad**. The larger screen is especially pleasant for the SBF See navigation tasks, because you can keep the task and the solution in view at the same time. On iPad the app is optimised for portrait mode.

## Conclusion

With the iOS version you can now use BoatPass on iPhone, iPad and Android. Content and features are the same on both platforms: the official question catalogue with explanations, mock exams with official papers, a study plan and the navigation tasks. The only difference: a purchase only applies to the store you bought it in.

Want to get started right away? Get **BoatPass** via [boatpass.de/en/app](/en/app/) and start for free with 15 questions a day. Once you know the app suits you, unlock the licence you need, paid once and with no subscription.
