When you start researching the German boating license (Sportbootführerschein), you quickly encounter an important question: Inland or Coastal?

## SBF Inland (Binnen)

The SBF Inland licenses you to operate motorboats on **inland waterways** — rivers, lakes, and canals in Germany.

**For:** Those wanting to boat on the Rhine, Elbe, Moselle, Lake Constance, or other German inland waters.

## SBF Coastal (See)

The SBF Coastal licenses you to operate motorboats in **coastal waters** up to 12 nautical miles from the coast.

**For:** Those wanting to boat on the North Sea, Baltic Sea, Mediterranean, or other coastal waters.

## Taking Both Together

Many exam providers offer a combined course for both licenses. This is often cheaper and more efficient.

## Which one should I get?

| | SBF Inland | SBF Coastal |
|---|---|---|
| Valid on | Rivers, lakes, canals | Coastal waters |
| Exam questions | 30 (min. 24 correct) | 30 (min. 24 correct) |
| Practical exam | Yes | Yes |
| Cost | ~€100–150 | ~€100–150 |

**Bottom line:** If you're planning coastal holidays, get the SBF Coastal. For German rivers and lakes, the SBF Inland is sufficient. Want both? Combine them.
