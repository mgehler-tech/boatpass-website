One of the most common questions before starting: how long do I actually need to study?

## The short answer

**4–8 weeks** at 30 minutes per day.

## Learner types and time needed

### Fast learner (3–4 weeks)
- Has prior technical knowledge (sailing license, motorcycle, etc.)
- Studies 45–60 minutes daily
- Uses targeted weak spot training

### Average learner (5–7 weeks)
- No prior knowledge needed
- 30 minutes daily is sufficient
- Plans 1–2 full mock exams at the end

### Casual learner (8–12 weeks)
- Studies 3–4 times per week
- Needs more time for nautical categories

## What takes the most time?

These categories typically take the most time:
1. **Seamanship** — knots, maneuvers, anchoring
2. **Navigation** — traffic rules, lights, signs
3. **Engine knowledge** — technical understanding required

## Tip

Start with the hardest categories. At the end, you'll have time for the easier areas and can walk into the exam relaxed.
